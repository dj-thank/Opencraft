# Code of Conduct

OpenCraft aims to be a welcoming project for artists, builders, researchers, beginners, and engineers.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique designs and code rather than people.
- Make room for contributors who are new to Blender, AI, networking, or open source.
- Respect privacy, consent, ownership, attribution, and accessibility.
- Do not pressure people to enable microphones, share private worlds, disclose prompts, or grant agent permissions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation, doxxing, non-consensual recording, malicious world destruction, credential theft, or publishing private reports is not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close participation, revoke access, or escalate serious safety concerns. Reports should be handled with minimum necessary disclosure.
